Draws

Random draws for the league, from pick order to the group stage.

Pick order draw Choose who is playing. Order and lottery balls follow the current table: the weakest draws the most balls, and the lowest number picks first.
Pairs draw Pick four competitors. The two higher on the table form pot 1, the two lower pot 2. Each pair takes one from each pot, with even odds.
Tournament draw One unplayed ad hoc tournament is drawn at random from the sheet, with even odds.
